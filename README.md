# Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't

One-line weather forecast for the invented city of Wobbleford — sun by 7:40, the 8:05 bus on time, dry shoes, hot coffee, and the last window seat with your name on it.

**Entry (127 characters):**

> In Wobbleford, sun by 7:40 and the 8:05 bus runs on time — dry shoes, hot coffee, and the last window seat has your name on it.

Built for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e). `main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes the line to `entry.txt`.
